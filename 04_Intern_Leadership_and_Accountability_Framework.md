## 2. The 4-Week Rotational Learning Curriculum

To keep interns engaged and prevent boredom, rotate interns through all 4 operational disciplines over a 4-week cycle:

```
[ Week 1: Digital Triage ] ──► [ Week 2: Farm Floor ] ──► [ Week 3: Post-Ops ] ──► [ Week 4: Client Ops ]
     (Slicing & Mesh)           (Hardware & Leveling)         (QC & Packing)           (Sales & CRM)
```

### Week 1: Digital Slicing & Mesh Engineering
* **Skills Mastered:** Cura, PrusaSlicer, Microsoft 3D Builder, Meshmixer.
* **Daily Output:** Slicing incoming client STL/OBJ files, orienting for optimal layer tensile strength, calculating weight/infill, and generating quotes in `index.html`.

### Week 2: Farm Floor Operation & Machine Telemetry
* **Skills Mastered:** Bed tramming, Z-offset calibration, thermal runaway diagnosis, extruder tear-down, cold pulls, nozzle replacement.
* **Daily Output:** Starting morning print queues on Printers 01–10, loading ABS/TPU/PLA spools, logging machine uptime.

### Week 3: Post-Processing, Surface Finishing & QC
* **Skills Mastered:** Support removal without gouging, heat-gun smoothing, chemical smoothing (acetone vapor for ABS), digital vernier caliper tolerance verification (±0.2mm).
* **Daily Output:** Measuring finished parts, logging scrap/failure rates, packing in bubble wrap with printable invoices.

### Week 4: Client Relationship Management (CRM) & Campus Outreach
* **Skills Mastered:** B2B communication, handling customer objections, WhatsApp Business automation.
* **Daily Output:** Executing college WhatsApp group blitzes, pitching student robotics clubs, coordinating Porter/Dunzo deliveries.

## 3. Client Intellectual Property (IP) & Non-Disclosure (NDA)

Clients—especially engineering final-year project teams and hardware startups at IITM Research Park—frequently upload confidential, patent-pending designs. **A breach of client confidentiality will destroy CarbonPrints' reputation.**

### Mandatory Intern Agreement Clauses:
1. **Zero Client CAD Distribution:** Interns are strictly prohibited from copying, downloading to personal drives, or sharing client STL/CAD files. Files reside strictly on the lab workdesk machine.
2. **Social Media Content Approval:** Interns cannot record videos of prototype prints unless the client has explicitly signed off on public showcase in writing.
3. **Internal Logbook Requirement:** All client files are assigned an anonymous internal ID (`CP-2026-XXXX`) so personal client data is protected.

## 5. Daily 15-Minute Standup Protocol

Hold a rapid, daily standup every morning at **09:30 AM IST** (in-person at the farm or via a 15-min Google Meet/WhatsApp audio call):

```
       [ 09:30 - 09:45 AM DAILY STANDUP AGENDA ]
                          │
  ┌───────────────────────┼───────────────────────┐
  ▼                       ▼                       ▼
1. YESTERDAY            2. TODAY                3. BLOCKERS
• Completed prints      • Scheduled print queue • Filament low?
• Revenue closed        • Urgent client jobs    • Nozzle clogged?
• Scrap / failures      • Campus visits         • Late client replies?
```

*Rule:* Standup must never exceed 15 minutes. It sets the momentum for the 10 printers and keeps every intern accountable.
