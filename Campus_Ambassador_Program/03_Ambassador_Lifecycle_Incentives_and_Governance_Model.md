# Campus Ambassador Lifecycle, Incentives & Governance Model

## Overview

A successful campus ambassador program is not a static list of names; it is a dynamic, high-engagement lifecycle. Without a structured journey, student enthusiasm decays within 3 to 4 weeks. 

This operational document details the **end-to-end ambassador lifecycle**, the **gamified 3-tier advancement ladder**, the **granular task & reward economy**, and the **accountability/governance protocols** to ensure high performance and zero administrative chaos.

### Stage 1: Recruitment, Screening & Selection (Days -14 to 0)

1. **Application Intake:**
   * Open applications 3 weeks before major academic project semesters (e.g., July–August for Fall semester, December–January for Spring capstone season).
   * Form requires: Academic year, Department, Club memberships, LinkedIn/Instagram handle, and a 100-word response: *"How would you introduce industrial 3D printing to your college's mechanical or robotics club?"*
2. **Screening Rubric (Scored out of 100 Points):**
   * *Campus Connectivity (40 pts):* Active in SAE, IEEE, Robotics, Maker labs, or Student Council.
   * *Technical Affinity (30 pts):* Basic understanding of CAD, prototyping, or hardware projects.
   * *Communication & Initiative (30 pts):* Proactive tone, clean formatting, previous event leadership.
3. **The 10-Minute Telephonic/Video Vetting Call:**
   * Confirm they have 3–4 hours per week of bandwidth.
   * Confirm their college has active project requirements (capstone projects, labs, student teams).
   * Evaluate clarity, poise, and genuine excitement.
4. **Offer & Agreement Execution:**
   * Selected candidates receive an official **Letter of Appointment** and a digital **Student Ambassador Agreement** requiring electronic signature before onboarding.

---

### Stage 2: Induction, Welcome Kit & The 14-Day Activation Sprint (Days 1 to 14)

1. **Virtual Cohort Induction (45 Minutes):**
   * Live orientation call covering company mission, additive manufacturing capabilities, slicing demo, coupon attribution rules, and communication protocols.
2. **Physical Welcome Showcase Kit Dispatch:**
   * Dispatched to the ambassador's hostel or home address via courier:
     * Official CarbonPrints Ambassador Lanyard & Identity Card.
     * High-tolerance mechanical showcase print (e.g., functional planetary gearbox or multi-color gyroid).
     * Material sample ring (PLA, ABS, PETG, TPU, Carbon Fiber PETG).
     * 5x A3 High-Gloss Campus Posters with their custom QR code.
     * Laptop sticker pack.
3. **The "First-14-Days" Activation Sprint:**
   * To prevent "phantom signups", ambassadors must complete 2 basic tasks within 14 days of receiving their kit:
     * *Task A:* Take a selfie with their Welcome Kit and post an announcement on LinkedIn: *"Proud to be appointed as Campus Ambassador for CarbonPrints Private Limited at [College Name]..."*
     * *Task B:* Pin one A3 poster on their department notice board OR introduce CarbonPrints to their college robotics/Baja/capstone team lead.
   * *Outcome:* Ambassadors who complete the sprint receive **100 Bonus Ambassador Points**; those who take zero action within 14 days receive a friendly check-in before potential seat reassignment.

### Stage 4: Graduation, Succession & Alumni Network (End of Tenure)

1. **Term Duration:** Each cohort operates on a 6-month semester-aligned cycle.
2. **Successor Grooming:** Outstanding ambassadors earn bonus credits by nominating and mentoring their 2nd-year replacement before stepping down.
3. **Graduation Deliverables:**
   * Official **Certificate of Program Completion & Excellence**.
   * Customized **Letter of Recommendation (LOR)** detailing empirical achievements (e.g., *"Facilitated ₹65,000 in rapid prototyping runs, organized 2 technical CAD seminars, led 14 student teams"*).
   * Lifetime 15% alumni discount on personal 3D printing orders.
   * Preferred routing into CarbonPrints engineering, operations, and sales internship roles.

## 3. Governance, Account Audits & Ethics Policy

### 3.1. Inactivity & Churn Management (The "30-Day Rule")
To maintain high standards, ambassadors must meet a basic activity floor:
* **The Threshold:** Ambassadors must log at least 1 verified activity (social post, poster verification, customer lead, or order) every 30 days.
* **Escalation Protocol:**
  * *Day 25 Inactivity Notice:* Friendly WhatsApp check-in from the Community Lead to ask if exams are interfering.
  * *Day 35 Inactivity Notice:* Formal warning notice offering 7 days to complete an active task.
  * *Day 42 De-provisioning:* If no response is received, the ambassador is respectfully decommissioned:
    * Referral coupon code deactivated.
    * Removed from internal community channels.
    * Forfeiture of unearned completion certificate and LOR.

### 3.2. Ethics, IP & Anti-Fraud Standards
* **Self-Referral Abuse:** Ambassadors cannot use their own referral code to purchase commercial inventory for resale without disclosure. Personal project orders receive direct ambassador quotas.
* **Client CAD Confidentiality (Strict Zero-Leak Policy):**
  * Ambassadors must never store, upload to public AI tools, or share client 3D files.
  * Any breach of client intellectual property triggers immediate termination, forfeiture of pending commissions, and potential legal action under the Indian Information Technology Act and Indian Contract Act.
* **No Unsolicited Spam:** Ambassadors are strictly barred from spamming non-affiliated college groups, auto-botting LinkedIn connections, or sending unsolicited cold DMs. Marketing must remain organic, value-additive, and professional.
