## STUDENT AMBASSADOR ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Student Ambassador Engagement Agreement ("**Agreement**") is entered into as of the date of digital acceptance or physical execution by and between:

1. **CARBONPRINTS PRIVATE LIMITED**, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India (hereinafter referred to as the "**Company**", which expression shall unless repugnant to the context include its successors, affiliates, and permitted assigns); and
2. **THE UNDERSIGNED STUDENT APPLICANT**, whose full legal name, college institution, department, and permanent residence details are set forth in Schedule A of this Agreement (hereinafter referred to as the "**Ambassador**" or "**Student Fellow**").

The Company and the Ambassador are individually referred to as a "**Party**" and collectively as the "**Parties**".

---

### PREAMBLE

WHEREAS, the Company operates an industrial additive manufacturing, rapid prototyping, and 3D printing farm in Chennai, catering to engineering students, hardware startups, makers, and research institutions across Tamil Nadu; and

WHEREAS, the Ambassador desires to act as an honorary campus brand advocate, technology liaison, DFAM (Design for Additive Manufacturing) educator, and community builder within their university campus to facilitate peer learning and drive prototyping adoption;

WHEREAS, the Company and Ambassador acknowledge that this arrangement is designed to be mutually beneficial — equipping the Ambassador with free prototyping credits, career credentials, and cash commissions while enabling the Company to build authentic campus-level relationships with its highest-value customer segment;

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual promises, access to industrial training, performance-based commissions, free prototyping quotas, and the credentialing benefits described herein, the Parties agree as follows:

---

### 1. Scope of Honorary Appointment & Independent Representative Status

1.1. **Non-Employment Clarification:** The Ambassador enters into this Agreement strictly as an **independent honorary student brand ambassador**. This Agreement does NOT constitute, establish, or imply any employment, partnership, joint venture, agency, franchise, or official legal representation.

1.2. **No Labor Claims:** The Ambassador is not eligible for statutory employment benefits, including but not limited to Employee Provident Fund (EPF), Employee State Insurance (ESI), minimum wage schedules, gratuity, severance, or health insurance under Indian labor law.

1.3. **Academic Primacy Guarantee:** The Company formally affirms that the Ambassador's academic commitments, examinations, university placements, and university regulations shall take absolute precedence over all Ambassador activities. Participation is strictly extracurricular and voluntary (estimated 3 to 4 hours per week maximum). The Ambassador shall never be pressured or penalized for academic prioritization.

1.4. **Standby Mode Right:** The Ambassador shall have the right to self-declare "Standby Mode" for up to six (6) continuous weeks per semester (typically during university examination periods in November and April–May) by notifying the Company's Community Lead via WhatsApp. During Standby, the minimum activity requirements of Section 6.3 are suspended, and referral coupon codes remain active.

---

### 2. Authorized Activities & Scope of Representation

2.1. **Permitted Ambassador Activities:**
* Introducing peers, student project teams, robotics societies, and SAE collegiate chapters (Baja, Formula Student, Robocon, Formula Bharat) to CarbonPrints prototyping services through peer-to-peer value-add communication.
* Sharing designated informational posters, technical slicing tutorials, authorized discount referral codes (`CP-AMB-[COLLEGE]-[ID]`), and free CAD DFM review offers.
* Organizing sanctioned technical seminars and CAD/DFAM workshops in coordination with department student associations, AICTE IDEA Lab coordinators, or faculty advisors.
* Distributing authorized material sample swatches and physical showcase parts provided in the official Welcome Kit.
* Formally pitching CarbonPrints as a "Club Manufacturing Partner" to SAE, Baja, Robocon, Drone, and Formula Student team captains, and negotiating bulk club discount agreements within the pre-authorized pricing guidelines (see Section 4.1).
* Publishing pre-approved social media content (LinkedIn posts, Instagram Reels) using the CarbonPrints Canva Brand Kit on their personal professional channels.

2.2. **Prohibited Activities & Negative Covenants:**
* **No Price Authority:** The Ambassador shall NOT quote arbitrary pricing, offer unapproved credit terms, or modify standard slicing rates. All binding price quotes are generated solely via the Company's authorized workdesk portal (`index.html`).
* **No Delivery Guarantees:** The Ambassador shall NOT commit the Company to rigid delivery windows without prior confirmation from the active farm operations manager.
* **No Binding Commitments:** The Ambassador holds no legal authority to execute contracts, MoUs, financial liabilities, or legal settlements on behalf of CarbonPrints Private Limited. All formal Club Partnership MoUs require Founder counter-signature.
* **No Mass Unsolicited Broadcast:** The Ambassador shall NOT mass-blast promotional content in WhatsApp groups where they have not established at least 14 days of active, non-promotional participation and at least 3 prior value-add contributions.
* **No Competitor Defamation:** The Ambassador shall not make disparaging, false, or unverifiable claims against competing prototyping services, college labs, or peer institutions.

---

### 3. Intellectual Property (IP) Protection & Strict Client CAD Non-Disclosure (NDA)

3.1. **Criticality of Client IP:** The Ambassador acknowledges that engineering students, capstone teams, Formula Student vehicle teams, and startup researchers frequently upload proprietary, patent-pending, and commercially sensitive CAD files (STL, OBJ, STEP, 3MF, F3D). The preservation of absolute confidentiality is of the highest legal and ethical importance.

3.2. **Strict Zero-Retention Policy:**
* The Ambassador shall NEVER copy, duplicate, screen-record, email to personal accounts, download to USB drives, or reverse-engineer any 3D model submitted by a fellow student or client.
* All client files must be routed directly into the designated secure CarbonPrints intake channel (official WhatsApp Desk or web workdesk `index.html`). The Ambassador shall not accept or forward client files through their personal WhatsApp or email.
* The Ambassador is strictly barred from feeding client designs or proprietary STL files into public artificial intelligence platforms, generative 3D model repositories, or any cloud service not operated by CarbonPrints.

3.3. **Company IP Ownership:** All trademarks, logos, brand guides, slicer profiles, workshop presentation slides, marketing collateral, Canva Brand Kit assets, and poster designs provided to the Ambassador remain the exclusive intellectual property of CarbonPrints Private Limited. The Ambassador receives a revocable, non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use such collateral solely for authorized campus advocacy during the tenure of this Agreement.

3.4. **Data Protection:** The Ambassador shall handle all peer student contact information (WhatsApp numbers, emails, CAD files) with strict discretion and shall not compile, sell, or share peer contact data for any purpose beyond facilitating CarbonPrints orders.

---

### 4. Attribution, Commission Economy & Disbursement Rules

4.1. **Attribution Mechanism:** Commissions and milestone points are credited exclusively when an end-client enters the Ambassador's unique alphanumeric code (`CP-AMB-[COLLEGE]-[ID]`) upon order confirmation or invoice generation in `index.html`. Self-referral on personal project quota orders is explicitly permitted and does not earn cash commission (it deducts from the print quota instead).

4.2. **Commission Rate Structure (Tier-Linked):**

| Ambassador Tier | Commission Rate | Unlock Threshold |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Tier 1 – Campus Cadet | 5% of net pre-tax order value | Entry (upon kit activation) |
| Tier 2 – Campus Lead | 7% of net pre-tax order value | ₹20,000 cumulative campus billing OR 500 Ambassador Points |
| Tier 3 – Regional Fellow | 10% of net pre-tax order value | ₹75,000 cumulative billing OR 1,500 Ambassador Points |
| Express Order Bonus | Additional 2% on top of tier rate | For orders using the "CarbonPrints Express" (same-day) service tier |

4.3. **Free Prototyping Quotas:** Material quotas earned (in grams of PLA/PETG/ABS/TPU) are non-transferable, carry zero cash surrender value, and must be redeemed for the Ambassador's personal or academic capstone engineering projects during their active tenure. Unused quotas lapse 30 days after tenure end.

4.4. **Disbursement Modality & Timeline:**
* Cash commissions are calculated on a monthly calendar cycle.
* Disbursed via Unified Payments Interface (UPI) or direct NEFT bank transfer within **seven (7) business days** following month-end.
* Minimum payout threshold: **₹500** (Five Hundred Indian Rupees). Commissions below ₹500 carry forward to the next cycle.
* Commission disbursement is contingent on: (a) the referred client's payment being fully settled (not pending), and (b) no active fraud dispute on the attributed order.

4.5. **TDS Disclosure:** As CarbonPrints is a registered business entity, commission payouts above the statutory threshold may attract Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) under Section 194H of the Income Tax Act, 1961. The Company will issue Form 16A to the Ambassador for all TDS deducted during the financial year.

---

### 5. Code of Conduct, Academic Integrity & Anti-Spam Policy

5.1. **The Anti-Spam Protocol (3-Layer Verification Before Any Group Post):**
Before posting promotional content in any WhatsApp group, Discord server, or Telegram channel, the Ambassador must verify:
* (a) They have been an **active member** of that group for at least 14 days prior to any promotional post.
* (b) They have contributed **at least 3 non-promotional, value-add messages** to that group.
* (c) The group admin has **implicitly or explicitly permitted** the posting of service-related announcements.

5.2. **Value-Add Communication Standard:** All campus outreach must follow the "Help First, Offer Second" principle. An Ambassador who is recognized by peers as a genuinely helpful technical resource — not a commercial agent — converts at 5–10x the rate of one who mass-blasts promotional messages.

5.3. **Truth in Advertising:** The Ambassador shall never make false, misleading, or exaggerated claims regarding material mechanical tolerances, tensile strength, turnaround speeds, or pricing. Use only figures from the official spec sheets and `index.html` pricing calculator.

5.4. **Zero Brand Disparagement:** The Ambassador shall maintain a professional demeanor and shall not engage in slanderous or defamatory statements against competing 3D printing services, faculty members, university labs, or peer institutions.

5.5. **AI & Content Authenticity:** All social media content posted using the CarbonPrints brand identity must represent genuine, verifiable experiences. AI-generated fake testimonials, fake order screenshots, or fabricated peer reviews are grounds for immediate termination.

---

### 6. Term, Standby Mode, Inactivity & Termination

6.1. **Standard Term:** This Agreement remains in effect for six (6) months from the execution date. It may be extended for an additional semester upon mutual written consent and satisfaction of minimum Tier 1 activity requirements.

6.2. **Termination for Convenience:** Either Party may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, by providing seven (7) days written notice via email or official WhatsApp communication to the designated Company contact.

6.3. **Inactivity Clause (The 30-Day Activity Floor):**

| Timeline | Trigger | Company Response | Ambassador Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Day 25 of inactivity | 0 verified activities logged | Friendly WhatsApp check-in from Community Lead | None — check-in only |
| Day 35 of inactivity | Still 0 activities | Formal warning notice; 7-day cure period offered | Referral code temporarily suspended |
| Day 42 of inactivity | No response or cure | Respectful decommissioning via WhatsApp | Code deactivated; LOR/Certificate forfeited |

*Note: Standby Mode (Section 1.4) pauses this clock for declared examination periods.*

6.4. **Immediate Termination for Breach:** The Company reserves the right to immediately terminate this Agreement, deactivate all codes, and forfeit all accrued but unpaid commissions in the event of:
* Breach of Client CAD Confidentiality or IP violation (Section 3).
* Harassment, fraudulent self-dealing, or unethical conduct toward peers or clients.
* Misappropriation of Company assets or funds.
* Verified violation of the Anti-Spam Protocol (Section 5.1) causing reputational damage to CarbonPrints in any campus community.

---

### 7. Post-Termination Obligations

Upon termination or expiration of this Agreement:
* The Ambassador shall immediately cease representing themselves as an **active** Campus Ambassador for CarbonPrints.
* All assigned promotional discount codes shall be deactivated within 48 hours of termination notice.
* The Ambassador may retain physical promotional items (lanyards, stickers, showcase prints) as personal keepsakes but must not use them for unauthorized promotional initiatives.
* All confidentiality and IP non-disclosure obligations set forth in Section 3 shall **survive the termination of this Agreement indefinitely**.
* **Lifetime Alumni Benefits (upon successful completion only):** Ambassadors who complete the full 6-month term in good standing retain: (a) 15% lifetime alumni discount on personal orders, (b) access to the CarbonPrints alumni WhatsApp group, and (c) priority routing for summer engineering apprenticeship roles.

---

### 8. Dispute Resolution & Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the substantive laws of the Republic of India. In the event of any dispute, difference, or claim arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, the Parties shall:
1. First attempt to resolve the matter amicably through direct WhatsApp/email communication within 15 days.
2. If unresolved, engage in informal mediation facilitated by a mutually agreed neutral academic faculty member or industry professional.
3. If still unresolved, the courts of competent jurisdiction located in **Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India**, shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
