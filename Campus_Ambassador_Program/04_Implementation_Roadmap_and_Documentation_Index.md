## 1. The 6-Week Phased Launch Roadmap

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        6-WEEK OPERATIONAL LAUNCH TIMELINE                              │
├───────────────┬───────────────┬───────────────┬────────────────┬───────────────┬───────┤
│ WEEK 1        │ WEEK 2        │ WEEK 3        │ WEEK 4         │ WEEK 5        │ WEEK 6│
│ Setup & Build │ Application   │ Candidate     │ Kit 3D Print   │ Induction &   │ Live  │
│ Documentation │ Recruitment   │ Screening &   │ & Courier      │ 14-Day Sprint │ Sprint│
│ & Legal Rails │ Campaign      │ Vetting Calls │ Dispatch       │ Kickoff       │ Orders│
└───────────────┴───────────────┴───────────────┴────────────────┴───────────────┴───────┘
```

---

### Week 1: Infrastructure, Legal Rails & Asset Staging
* Finalize and sign off on core foundation documents (`01`, `02`, `03`, `04`).
* Build public application form on Tally.so / Google Forms.
* Set up internal Discord/WhatsApp community server with dedicated channels (`#announcements`, `#sprint-tasks`, `#order-wins`, `#helpdesk`).
* Create central Google Drive asset folder containing high-res logos, poster designs, and sample STL files.

### Week 2: Targeted Recruitment Campaign (The "Pilot 10" Hubs)
* Announce the **CarbonPrints Student Fellowship / Ambassador Program** across LinkedIn and targeted student WhatsApp networks.
* Target primary pilot institutions (e.g., IIT Madras, Anna University CEG/MIT, SSN College of Engineering, VIT Chennai, SRM Kattankulathur, Sathyabama, Rajalakshmi).
* Set target intake: 10 to 15 elite ambassadors for Cohort 1.

### Week 3: Screening, Vetting Calls & Appointment Issuance
* Screen incoming applications using the 100-point scoring rubric.
* Conduct 10-minute vetting calls with top 20 candidates.
* Select top 12 ambassadors across target hubs.
* Issue digital **Letter of Appointment** and obtain signed **Student Ambassador Agreement**.

### Week 4: Welcome Kit Production & Dispatch
* 3D printer farm runs dedicated production for Welcome Kit items:
  * 12x functional planetary gearbox or multi-color gyroid mechanical prints.
  * 12x filament material swatch rings (PLA, ABS, PETG, TPU, CF-PETG).
* Print lanyards, ID badges, and A3 posters with unique QR codes.
* Pack and dispatch physical kits via local courier/speed post to ambassador addresses.

### Week 5: Virtual Cohort Induction & 14-Day Sprint
* Host 45-minute live virtual induction for Cohort 1.
* Distribute custom referral coupon codes (e.g., `CP-AMB-SSN-SK`).
* Launch the **"First-14-Days Activation Sprint"** (LinkedIn appointment announcement + notice board poster deployment).
* Introduce the weekly sprint cadence.

### Week 6 and Ongoing: Live Field Execution & Weekly Sprints
* First campus prototyping orders flow into the `index.html` workdesk.
* Automated attribution logs 5%–10% commission and points.
* Weekly async Monday briefing and Friday PoW submission.
* Monthly revenue leaderboard and top ambassador shout-out.
