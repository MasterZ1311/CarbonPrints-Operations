## 4. Corporate Banking & Revenue Flow Architecture

All customer funds flow strictly through the company's verified corporate account:

### 4.1 Current Account Operation
* **Account Name:** `CarbonPrints Private Limited`
* **Bank Requirement:** Opened with Certificate of Incorporation, PAN, MOA/AOA, and Board Resolution authorizing designated Directors as signatories.
* **Zero-MDR Merchant UPI:** Direct merchant integration via Google Pay Business / Paytm Enterprise linked to the current account for zero-fee instant customer settlements.

### 4.2 Corporate Income Tax Structure
* Under corporate tax reforms, domestic manufacturing companies in India are taxed under:
  - **Section 115BAA:** Concessional corporate tax rate of **22%** (+10% surcharge + 4% cess = **25.17% effective**).
* **Advance Tax Schedule:**
  - June 15: 15% of estimated tax.
  - September 15: 45% of estimated tax.
  - December 15: 75% of estimated tax.
  - March 15: 100% of estimated tax.
